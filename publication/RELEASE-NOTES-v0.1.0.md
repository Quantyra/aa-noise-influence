**Noise smoothing sharpens a quantitative Aaronson-Ambainis influence bound**

Daniel Fredriksen · Quantyra Inc. · ORCID 0009-0002-9123-7435

This short note derives the general bound

```text
max_i Inf_i(f) >= sqrt(45)/(16000 pi^4) * Var(f)^(5/2)/d^(11/2)
```

for every real multilinear polynomial of degree at most `d >= 1`, bounded by one on the uniform sign cube, with positive variance. Influence uses squared half-edge differences.

The result is a quantitative corollary combining Turturean's influence theorem with classical Bernstein smoothing. The manuscript attributes the row-bound and smoothing antecedents, records every normalization and variance loss, and derives an explicit sufficient average-input query bound. It does not claim an independent AA proof, polynomial-time classical simulation, BPP/BQP resolution, independent peer review, formal certification or established publication priority.
